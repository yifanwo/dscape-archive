RECORDS

Physical media carrying the early digital catalog.

Act II · Local Infrastructure

SYSTEMS

The hardware and software running the library.

Building the digital library required localized infrastructure. Installation discs updated core systems, KVM switches managed terminal access, and DAT tapes ensured nightly server backups.

Act III · Connected Era

NETWORKS

Transitioning from local hardware to connected infrastructure.

The dawn of the network era changed everything. IP cameras watched over the lab, while early NAS devices centralized files, bridging isolated workstations and shared digital spaces.
